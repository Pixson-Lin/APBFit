# APBFit Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-30
**Last updated:** 2026-06-30

This Privacy Policy explains how the **APBFit** application ("APBFit", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. APBFit is distributed for sideload / testing use and is **not** an official Google Play production release at this time.

**Contact:** pixson.lin@gmail.com

---

## 1. Summary

- APBFit runs **entirely on your device**. There is **no APBFit server** and **no APBFit-operated database**.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
- The only external service the app communicates with is **Google** (Google Sign-In and the Google Fit / Fitness API), in order to sign you in and to **write activity data into your own Google Fit account** at your request.
- All app-generated history is stored **locally** on your device and can be deleted by you at any time.

---

## 2. What APBFit does

APBFit lets a signed-in Google account generate and **write simulated walking/running activity** (step count, distance, and activity segments) into **Google Fit**. You configure the intensity, duration, and batching; the app then writes the corresponding records to your Google Fit account.

---

## 3. Information the app accesses and processes

### 3.1 Google account information (via Google Sign-In)
- Your Google account **email address** and **account identifier**, and basic profile information returned by Google Sign-In.
- Purpose: to identify which account(s) you have enabled and to perform Google Fit writes on their behalf.

### 3.2 Google Fit / fitness data (via the Fitness API)
- APBFit requests **write** permission for: step count, distance, and activity segments.
- The app **creates and writes** these fitness records to your Google Fit account. This is **health and fitness data**.
- APBFit does **not** read your existing Google Fit history for analytics or profiling.

### 3.3 Data stored locally on your device
- **Run history and segment records** (start/end times, generated steps/distance, write status) — stored in a local on-device database (Room).
- **App preferences** — selected accounts, run configuration (intensity, duration, batch size), and last-selected options — stored in local app storage (SharedPreferences/DataStore).
- This local data never leaves your device except as the fitness records you choose to write to Google Fit.

### 3.4 What we do NOT collect
- No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs.
- No location data.
- No transmission of your data to the developer or to any third-party server.

---

## 4. How information is used

Information is used solely to:
- Authenticate you with Google and maintain your enabled-account list.
- Generate and write the activity records you request into Google Fit.
- Display your run history and status within the app.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the features above.

---

## 5. Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

APBFit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the **Limited Use** requirements. Specifically:

- Google user data is used only to provide and improve the app's stated features (writing activity to your Google Fit).
- We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties, ad networks, data brokers, or for advertising.
- We do not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to the app's features.
- No humans read your Google user data except where required for security, to comply with law, or with your explicit consent.

---

## 6. Data storage, retention, and deletion

- App history is stored **locally** and is automatically pruned: records older than **90 days** are deleted on app launch.
- You can clear history within the app, and **uninstalling APBFit removes all local app data**.
- **Fitness records already written to Google Fit live in your Google account**, not in APBFit. To remove them, use the Google Fit app or your Google Account data controls.
- You can revoke APBFit's access at any time via your Google Account → **Security → Third-party access / connected apps**, or by signing out within the app.

---

## 7. Data sharing

We do **not** share your data with third parties. The app only exchanges data with **Google services** as necessary to sign you in and to write fitness records to your own Google Fit account.

---

## 8. Security

- APBFit relies on Android's app sandbox and Google's authenticated APIs.
- No credentials or passwords are stored by the app; authentication is handled by Google Sign-In / Google Play Services.

---

## 9. Children's privacy

APBFit is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect data from them.

---

## 10. Access restrictions (testing)

APBFit currently uses a Google OAuth configuration in **Testing** mode. Only Google accounts added as **test users** can sign in and use the Google Fit features.

---

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

---

## 12. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact: pixson.lin@gmail.com.

## 1. 摘要

- APBFit **完全在你的裝置上執行**，**沒有 APBFit 伺服器**、**沒有我們營運的資料庫**。
- 我們**不會**販售、出租或將你的資料分享給第三方。
- 本 App 唯一會通訊的外部服務是 **Google**（Google 登入與 Google Fit／Fitness API），用途是讓你登入，以及依你的指示把活動資料**寫入你自己的 Google Fit 帳號**。
- 所有由 App 產生的歷史紀錄都**儲存在你的裝置本機**，你可隨時刪除。

---

## 2. APBFit 的功能

APBFit 讓已登入的 Google 帳號產生並**寫入模擬的步行／跑步活動**（步數、距離、活動區段）到 **Google Fit**。你可設定強度、時長與批次量，App 會據此把對應紀錄寫入你的 Google Fit 帳號。

---

## 3. App 存取與處理的資訊

### 3.1 Google 帳號資訊（透過 Google 登入）
- 你的 Google 帳號 **email**、**帳號識別碼**，以及 Google 登入回傳的基本個人資料。
- 用途：辨識你啟用的帳號，並代表這些帳號執行 Google Fit 寫入。

### 3.2 Google Fit／健身資料（透過 Fitness API）
- APBFit 申請步數、距離、活動區段的**寫入**權限。
- App 會**建立並寫入**這些健身紀錄到你的 Google Fit 帳號。此屬於**健康與健身資料**。
- APBFit **不會**讀取你既有的 Google Fit 歷史用於分析或建檔。

### 3.3 儲存在裝置本機的資料
- **Run 歷史與 segment 紀錄**（起訖時間、產生的步數／距離、寫入狀態）— 儲存於裝置本機資料庫（Room）。
- **App 偏好設定** — 選取的帳號、Run 設定（強度、時長、批次量）、上次選項 — 儲存於本機 App 儲存空間（SharedPreferences／DataStore）。
- 這些本機資料不會離開你的裝置，除了你選擇寫入 Google Fit 的健身紀錄之外。

### 3.4 我們不會蒐集的資料
- 不含分析、廣告識別碼或追蹤 SDK。
- 不蒐集定位資料。
- 不會將你的資料傳送給開發者或任何第三方伺服器。

---

## 4. 資訊的使用方式

資訊僅用於：
- 透過 Google 驗證你的身分並維護你的啟用帳號清單。
- 依你的要求產生並寫入活動紀錄到 Google Fit。
- 在 App 內顯示你的 Run 歷史與狀態。

我們**不會**將你的資料用於廣告、建檔，或與上述功能無關的任何用途。

## 6. 資料儲存、保留與刪除

- 你可在 App 內清除歷史；**移除安裝 APBFit 會刪除所有本機 App 資料**。
- **已寫入 Google Fit 的健身紀錄存在你的 Google 帳號**中，不在 APBFit 內。若要刪除，請使用 Google Fit App 或你的 Google 帳號資料管理工具。
